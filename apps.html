Chrono

Sleep Tracker · iOS · 2026
View on the App Store ↗

Privacy-first sleep tracker I designed, built and shipped solo to the App Store.

  • SwiftUI + MVVM. For iPhone users, sleep is captured via the iOS 18 Sleep Focus Filter (App Intents). HealthKit is the only data store, no server.
  • Gamified the core loop around three chronotype companions (Deer, Bear, Panther) whose health evolves each night with the user's sleep score.
  • Designed a 16-step onboarding that takes users from a chronotype quiz to a fully configured, ready-to-use app.
  • Monetized with StoreKit 2 + RevenueCat; localized in 11 languages.

Tiny Goods

Coloring Books · iOS · 2026
View on the App Store ↗

Cozy coloring app I designed, built and shipped solo to the App Store.

  • Built in SwiftUI + PencilKit around a "real card" metaphor: a rotatable canvas and wavy-bordered pages.
  • Offline-first SwiftData gallery that shows finished pages as washi-taped polaroids instead of grid thumbnails.
  • Monetized with StoreKit 2 + RevenueCat + AdMob; product decisions driven by PostHog; localized in 11 languages.
